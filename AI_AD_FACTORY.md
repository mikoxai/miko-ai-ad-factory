# AI Ad Factory workflow

### 1. Connect

Start in Codex. Enter your Kie API key in the local setup form and connect Whop in chat.

### 2. Add your product and reference

Provide your product photos, offer, audience and reference video. You can also supply voice samples you have permission to use.

The research stage covers your brand, competitor offers, Meta Ad Library, Google Ads Transparency and Reddit. Review the findings and recommended angles before scripting.

### 3. Analyze the reference

Gemini and FFmpeg help break down the full video: dialogue, cuts, zooms, camera movement, product handling, B-roll and graphics.

### 4. Review your storyboard

Review each scene’s timing, dialogue, speaker, framing, product action, graphics and transitions. Approve the plan and generation costs before production.

### 5. Prepare assets

- **Characters:** described directly in the video prompts.
- **Voices:** optional samples assigned to individual speakers.
- **Products:** your product photos.
- **Graphics:** adapted cards and collages created with Nano Banana Pro.
- **Motion references:** selected excerpts when approved for the scene.

### 6. Generate

Seedance 2.5 creates the video through Kie using the approved script, scene plan and assets. Longer ads use connected clips. Dialogue captions are off by default.

### 7. Review and refine

Check the full ad against the storyboard. Refine cuts, framing, graphics, dialogue and transitions, keeping the original and revised versions.

### 8. Prepare your Whop campaign

Review the finished creative, headline, copy, destination, targeting and budget. Prepare a draft through connected Whop tools when supported, or finish setup in Whop. Approve funding and launch separately.

### 9. Improve with results

Use campaign data to review performance and choose new variations. Set a reporting schedule when you want ongoing updates.
