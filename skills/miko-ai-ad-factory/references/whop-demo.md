# Whop campaign handoff

Connection is inside chat through actual available Whop tools, not a compulsory terminal CLI onboarding. Login/identity/payment entry happens on Whop. Check business identity and Ads readiness separately; never infer “not connected” from an empty campaign list.

After the ad is accepted, show Whop's Ads comparison page briefly and the actual account setup. It documents agency accounts, no daily spend limit, bulk upload, payment-based attribution and direct support. Attribute priority bidding/lower CPM/fewer rejections to Whop; do not promise no bans or guaranteed performance. An existing Facebook page/Instagram may need connecting. No setup/monthly/platform fee is not free ad spend.

Official source: https://docs.whop.com/manage-your-business/growth-marketing/ads

Prepare a local campaign preview containing business/account identity, sales objective, product/destination, creative file references, headline/body/CTA, purchase event/pixel readiness, geography, budget/currency and draft status. Display this for approval. Discover supported tools/schema for creating a non-delivering campaign; do not assume every CLI/API capability is in the installed plugin. If safe draft creation is unavailable, keep the preview local and guide the user through the UI. Launch/funding requires separate explicit approval.

Show how copy/targeting/budget can be edited in native Whop UI. Do not claim a campaign is live from a local preview or accepted task. Confirm launch intent and exact campaign/budget before any delivering action.

For recurring reports, ask for the desired schedule/timezone and use a specified attribution window, spend, revenue and conversion count. Profit requires fees, refunds, COGS and creative cost. Recommend variations grounded in sufficient real data. No schedule, paid regeneration, pausing, scaling or publication without explicit authority. Never fabricate results.
